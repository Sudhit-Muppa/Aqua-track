Not a safety assessment

The field probe on this site is a demonstration model. It shows how our scoring works and is not a live sensor feed. Nothing on this site, including any grade we publish for a reach, tells you whether water is safe to drink, swim in, wade in or fish from. Our readings are taken by students with student equipment and have not been certified by any lab or agency.

For safety decisions, follow advisories from the Virginia Department of Health and the Virginia Department of Environmental Quality.

The map

Reach lines are hand-drawn approximations from our own route planning, not survey-grade data. A reach appearing on the map does not mean it is public land or safe to access. Respect private property and posted signs. Map tiles are © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.
