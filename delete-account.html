Delete your AquaTrack account

You can delete your account yourself in the app in under a minute, or ask us by email. Either way, everything tied to the account is removed.

In the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the list of what will be removed, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

You are signed out immediately and the account is gone. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email theaquatrackinitiative@gmail.com from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my AquaTrack account". We may reply to confirm it is really you. We delete the account and confirm back to you within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: display name, location, age, profile picture and verified status.
  • Every water reading and home water test you logged, with their photos, locations and meter readings.
  • Your problem reports, comments and likes.
  • Your verification request.
  • Your sign-in: email address and password. You will not be able to sign in again. You can create a new account later with the same email.

What is kept

  • Other members' problem reports that cited one of your readings keep the report; the link to your reading is removed.
  • If you reviewed other members' readings as a team member, those readings stay, without a record of who approved them.
  • Server logs for up to 30 days, and database backups that age out within 30 days.
  • Emails you sent us, until you ask us to delete those too.

Deleting only some things

You do not have to delete the whole account. In the app you can delete a single reading or home test from Profile, delete a post or comment from the community feed, and remove your profile picture from Profile. The privacy policy explains what we keep and why.
