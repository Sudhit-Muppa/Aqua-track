Privacy policy

Short version: this website does not ask for, store or sell any information about you. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies of its own.

Who we are

Aqua Track is a student water-quality project run by six high school students in Loudoun County, Virginia. This policy covers aquatrackproject.org. You can reach us at theaquatrackinitiative@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The interactive probe runs entirely in your browser, and the slider values you pick are never sent anywhere.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we will delete it if you ask.

Services the site loads

Like most websites, this one relies on a few outside services. Each one receives your IP address and basic browser details when your browser requests a file from it:

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs to deliver and protect it. Vercel privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • unpkg serves Leaflet, the open-source map library.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles in the watershed chart. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Links to Instagram take you to Instagram's own site, where its policies apply.

Cookies

We do not set cookies or use browser storage, and none of the services above need cookies to load these files. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can ask us to delete that message.

Deleting your information

To have anything you sent us deleted, email theaquatrackinitiative@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
