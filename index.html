Our Mission

Every day, we witness the troubling reality of pollution clouding our neighborhood lakes, a sight that compels us to act. Ignoring this decline is no longer an option, as the health of our local ecosystem hangs in the balance. We realized that legitimate change starts with us, so we made the decision to not just stand by, but to actively raise awareness about this critical situation. It is our responsibility to shine a light on these issues and inspire our community to protect the natural beauty that surrounds us.

We envision a future where our waters are returned to their natural state, shimmering with crystal clarity and completely free of harmful pollutants. Achieving this dream requires more than just physical cleanup efforts; it demands a fundamental shift in how we all interact with our environment. We are dedicated to teaching our neighbors that every individual has a vital role to play in saving our waterways. By coming together and taking ownership, we can ensure these precious resources remain vibrant and life-sustaining for generations to come.

About the Team

We are a team of 6 middle schoolers from Loudoun County who are trying to make a difference in the world around us. We are committed to doing our part to keep our local waterways clean and healthy. Our goal is not only to clean up the trash but to prove that anyone, regardless of age, can take charge and create a lasting positive impact on the environment.

Take Action

The health of our local ecosystem hangs in the balance, and it requires more than just passive observation—it demands immediate, hands-on action from people like you. The most powerful tool we have against pollution is your willingness to step up and physically restore our waterways.

Every piece of trash you remove prevents lasting harm to wildlife and protects the water quality we all rely on. Waiting for others to solve this problem is a luxury we simply cannot afford; the longer we wait, the more damage is done. By grabbing a bag and cleaning up your local river or lake, you are taking a definitive stand for your community's future. Your actions today send a powerful message that we will not accept a polluted environment, inspiring others to join the fight for a cleaner, healthier world.
