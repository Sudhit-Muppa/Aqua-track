Our Mission

Well, why are we doing this? We decided to start working to measure the levels of pollution in LOCAL ponds because we decided that simply not enough people are focusing on them. While many people debate about the overall issue with pollution in bay areas and reaching oceans, not enough talk about the main route cause. While we could remove pollution from oceans directly that would be unrealistic with the unimaginable amount of pollution that is actually in the seas. However, we decided to go straight to the source. While we could work on issues with air pollution, climate change, poverty & hunger, conflict & security issues, and threats to human rights & health, we decided that we need to focus on issues that remove long term issues and affect our day to day lives in a positive way. Simply put, we want to go straight to the source and remove pollution from local ponds to remove long term stress about pollution and water waste.

Our goal is simple: to spread awareness about the pollution in our local ponds while equipping you with the solutions to fix it. We want to give people specific, individual actions they can take to address the unique issues their neighborhood ponds face. Beyond just advice, we are developing devices that allow you to measure pollution and water quality levels yourself. By putting these tools in your hands, we hope to turn passive observation into active restoration.

About the Team

We are Aqua Track, and we are here to teach local communities about the real issues quickly arising issues with water pollution around us. We are a young group of middle school students who have noticed the issues with ponds in our local areas. Our group consists of Sreehan Bheemreddy, Sudhit Muppa, Pratheek Kumar, Ved Ayyagari, Yug Jindal, and Nihal Sripathi. Each of us has an extensive background consisting of leadership and engineering opportunities. Together, we have realized the issues with pollution in not only large river systems but also the pollution in our local ponds that can affect our day-to-day quality of life. Follow us on our journey as we focus on creating algorithms and machines that can help us evaluate the pollution levels of our local ponds. Let's promote the general welfare together.

Take Action

The health of our local ecosystem hangs in the balance, and it requires more than just passive observation—it demands immediate, hands-on action from people like you. The most powerful tool we have against pollution is your willingness to step up and physically restore our waterways.

Every piece of trash you remove prevents lasting harm to wildlife and protects the water quality we all rely on. Waiting for others to solve this problem is a luxury we simply cannot afford; the longer we wait, the more damage is done. By grabbing a bag and cleaning up your local river or lake, you are taking a definitive stand for your community's future. Your actions today send a powerful message that we will not accept a polluted environment, inspiring others to join the fight for a cleaner, healthier world.
