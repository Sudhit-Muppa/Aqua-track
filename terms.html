Terms of use

Draft. These terms are being reviewed before the AquaTrack app launches. They describe how the app and this website work today; the wording may still change.

AquaTrack is a student project shared for education. These terms cover aquatrackproject.org and the AquaTrack app for iOS and Android. By using either, you agree to them.

Who can use AquaTrack

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Give accurate information when you sign up, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if someone else gets into your account. One person, one account.

Not a safety assessment

The scores, bands and observations in the app come from a computer vision model reading a photo, plus whatever meter readings you type in. They are estimates for community awareness. The field probe on this site is a demonstration model. Nothing in the app or on this site, including any score or grade we publish, tells you whether water is safe to drink, swim in, wade in or fish from. Our readings are taken by students and volunteers with consumer equipment and have not been certified by any lab or agency.

For safety decisions, follow advisories from the Virginia Department of Health and the Virginia Department of Environmental Quality.

What you may not do

  • Submit readings, photos or reports you know to be false, staged, or taken somewhere other than where you say.
  • Trespass or ignore posted signs to reach water. A river on the map is not permission to go there.
  • Post photos of people, the inside of private property, or anything you do not have the right to share.
  • Harass, threaten or impersonate anyone in posts or comments.
  • Scrape the app or its server, run automated accounts, or try to get around the review process.
  • Upload anything illegal, or anything designed to damage the service or other people's devices.

When you delete a reading or your account, that content is removed; statistics already published may keep the aggregate numbers it contributed, with nothing that identifies you.

We may remove content that breaks these terms or the law, and we may decline to publish a reading without giving a reason.

Verification and review

Verified status is granted by the team after reading your request. It is a courtesy, not a right: we may refuse, delay or withdraw it. Every public river reading is reviewed by a team member before it appears in statistics or the community feed.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile in the app, or by email as described on the account deletion page. We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms.
