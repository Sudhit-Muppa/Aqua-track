Privacy policy

Draft. This policy is being reviewed before the AquaTrack app launches. It describes what the app and this website do today; the wording may still change.

Short version: this website collects nothing about you. The AquaTrack app needs an account, and it keeps the water photos, locations and readings you choose to submit so they can appear on your map and, once you are verified, be shared with other members. We do not sell any of it, and we do not run ads.

Who we are

Aqua Track is a student water-quality project run by six high school students in Loudoun County, Virginia. This policy covers aquatrackproject.org and the AquaTrack app for iOS and Android. You can reach us at theaquatrackinitiative@gmail.com.

The website

The site has no accounts, forms, analytics or cookies of its own. The interactive probe runs in your browser, and the slider values you pick are never sent anywhere. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we delete it if you ask.

Like most websites, this one loads a few files from outside services. Each one receives your IP address and basic browser details when your browser asks it for a file:

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs to deliver and protect it. Vercel privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • unpkg serves Leaflet, the open-source map library, and jsDelivr serves the Supabase library used only on the account page.
  • OpenStreetMap serves the map tiles in the watershed chart. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

The account page at aquatrackproject.org/auth.html finishes email confirmations and password resets for the app. It talks to Supabase, our sign-in provider, keeps nothing in your browser once it is done, and sets no cookies. Links to Instagram take you to Instagram's own site, where its policies apply.

The app: what you give us

  • Account. Your email address and password, a display name, the town or area you type in, and your age. Supabase stores the password as a hash; we never see it. Age is used to confirm you are 13 or older.
  • Water readings. The photo you take, the river you pick, the GPS location of the photo, any meter readings you type in (pH, temperature, turbidity and so on) and an optional caption.
  • Home water tests. The name and notes you give a private water body such as a tap or a well, and the readings you log for it.
  • Posts. Problem reports with a photo, a location and a description, and your comments and likes on other members' posts.
  • Profile picture. Optional. It is stored at a public web address so it can appear next to your posts.
  • Verification request. The full name, location and short statement you send when you ask to be verified. A team member reads it.

Automatically, our server records the IP address, device type and time of each request in standard logs, and the app keeps your sign-in token on your phone so you stay signed in.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your readings on your map, keep you signed in, and let you edit or delete what you submitted.
  • To score the water. Each photo, together with any meter readings you added and public historical data for that river, is sent through OpenRouter to a free AI vision model (currently Google's Gemma or NVIDIA's Nemotron), which returns the analysis you see in the app. The photo is sent without your name, email or account details. Free models are run by outside providers who may log what they receive and may use it to improve their models, so do not photograph people or anything private.
  • To answer river questions and search reference data, short text (never your photos) goes to Google's Gemini API.
  • To build river statistics: approved readings from verified members feed each river's score, trend and health distribution.
  • To review: team members read verification requests and approve public readings before they appear to others.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to send account emails such as confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not train AI models on it ourselves. The free model providers described above may use the photos they receive to improve their models.

Who can see what

Until you are verified, your readings and home tests are visible only to you and to the team members who review them. Nothing you submit appears in the public feed or in river statistics.

Once verified, your approved readings, problem reports, comments, display name, profile picture and verified mark are visible to every AquaTrack member, and the GPS pin of a reading or report is shown on the map. Your email address, age and password are never shown to anyone.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase runs the database, file storage and sign-in, in the United States. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud runs the AquaTrack server (Cloud Run, US East) and the Gemini API used for river questions and search. Google privacy policy.
  • OpenRouter routes each photo to a free AI vision model that scores it. The model provider receives the photo, any meter readings and river context, but not your name or email. OpenRouter privacy policy.
  • Esri and OpenStreetMap provide the map tiles in the app. Your phone requests tiles straight from Esri's servers, which see your IP address and the map area you are viewing. Esri privacy.

The apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

How long we keep it

Your readings, posts and profile stay until you delete them or your account. Deleting a reading or post removes it right away. Deleting your account removes everything listed on the account deletion page, including your photos and your sign-in. Server logs are kept for 30 days. Database backups age out within 30 days.

Children

You must be 13 or older to create an account. The sign-up form refuses younger ages, and we delete any account we learn belongs to a child under 13. If you are between 13 and 17, a parent or guardian can email us to see or delete your data, and we will act on it as if you had asked.

Your choices

  • Edit your display name, location and profile picture in Profile.
  • Location access is needed to log a reading. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time, which stops the app from reading your location.
  • Delete any reading, home test, post or comment from the app.
  • Delete your whole account from Profile, or by email, as described on the account deletion page.
  • Email us for a copy of the data we hold about you. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Traffic between the app, our server and Supabase is encrypted. Your sign-in token lives in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Only team members with a reason to review content can see unpublished readings. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will email you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. For a change that matters to you, we will also say so in the app.
